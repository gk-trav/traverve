*Privacy Policy for traverve.com*

Last updated: 07/08/2024

At Traverve, accessible from www.traverve.com, protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Traverve and how we use it.

*1. General Information*

Traverve is dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring a positive experience on our website. We do not collect any personal information from our users beyond what is necessary for the functionality and improvement of our services.

*2. No Log Policy*

Traverve does not capture or store any login activity, ensuring your use of our services remains private and secure.

*3. Information We Do Not Collect*

  • *Email Addresses*: Traverve does not collect email addresses.
  • *Phone Numbers*: Traverve does not collect phone numbers.
  • *Personal Identifiable Information*: We do not store or process any personal identifiable information beyond what is necessary for providing our services.

*4. Third-Party Services*

We utilize third-party services such as Firebase, Google Analytics, and Google Ads to enhance website functionality, manage affiliate tracking, analyze traffic, and optimize user interactions. We collect event data at an aggregate level, which does not personally identify any user.

*5. Use of Non-Personal Information*

We may collect non-personally identifiable information about you during your interaction with our site. This information may include technical information about the device or browser you use, as well as your interactions with our site and services. This data is used solely to improve the quality of our service and to provide a more personalized experience.

*6. Cookies*

Traverve may use cookies to enhance the user experience, such as maintaining session states or storing user preferences. We do not use cookies to track personal information.

*7. Redirect to Third-Party Brands*

Traverve redirects users to several brands for completing transactions. Once you leave our website to visit a third-party website, the privacy policy of that particular brand will apply to any information you provide or that is collected by the third-party.

*8. Changes to This Privacy Policy*

We reserve the right to update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

*9. Contact Us*

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • By visiting this page on our website: www.traverve.com/contact
